# Phantom Fence — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 23, 2026

Phantom Fence is a Windows utility published by Solemn Scribe. This policy is short because the app's data practices are simple: **Phantom Fence does not collect any data.**

## What the app does with data

- **No collection.** Phantom Fence does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, usage data, telemetry, or analytics — nothing, from anyone.
- **No network activity.** The app makes no network connections of any kind. It has no accounts, no sign-in, and no online components.
- **Local settings only.** Your preferences (which displays are fenced and which features are enabled) are saved in the Windows registry on your own computer, under your own user profile. This information never leaves your machine, and it is removed if you delete the app's registry key (`HKCU\Software\PhantomFence`).
- **No third parties.** Because no data is collected, no data is shared with, sold to, or processed by anyone.

## What the app can see, and why

To do its job, Phantom Fence uses standard Windows mechanisms to observe mouse movement, the Win+Shift+Arrow keyboard shortcut, and the positions of open windows on your screen. This happens entirely in memory on your computer, solely to keep windows and the cursor off displays you have fenced. None of it is recorded, logged, or transmitted.

## Purchases

If you obtain Phantom Fence through the Microsoft Store, Microsoft processes the transaction under its own privacy statement. Solemn Scribe receives no personal information from your purchase.

## Children

Phantom Fence is a general-purpose utility appropriate for all ages, and it collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If a future version of the app ever changes these practices, this policy will be updated and the effective date revised before that version is released.
